\T{2122.67}{1230}{30}{ink}{\bfseries Conclusion}
\Par{2122.67}{1276}{885.33}{24}{33}{ink}{A universal humanoid motion tracker can be ported to a small humanoid on a single GPU, without changing its network: SONIC-K1 tracks \nHeld\% of held-out motions with \nShareCompute\ of SONIC’s compute, and a short post-training with the robot’s real limits makes it hold up under delay and run on the real K1.}
